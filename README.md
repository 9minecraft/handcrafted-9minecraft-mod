# Handcrafted — Furnish Your Empty Base with Working Furniture

Handcrafted 9minecraft Mod fills your buildings with cozy, hand-made furniture. It adds over 250 new decorative blocks to help you design the perfect home. You get everything from chairs you can actually sit in to fancy beds and working storage cupboards. All the pieces match standard wood types and other building materials so they fit right into your base. The mod gives you tons of options to turn a simple wooden box into a beautiful, fully furnished house.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Terrarium Modding**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
